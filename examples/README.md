# Worked example

`microsite/` shows one way to fill `templates/product/PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and a decision record with real product judgment instead of empty scaffolding.

Use it to calibrate depth and tone during a kickoff — read it, do not copy it. The scope, numbers, and decisions are invented for a small, low-risk microsite. A larger or riskier product should go deeper on the sections that carry its actual risk, and stay just as short on the ones that do not.
